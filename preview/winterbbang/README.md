# Winterbbang representative HTML preview

- Generated from `scripts/winterbbang_render.py` design and the original 64-block copy pool.
- Pages: home + 6 regional representative pages (Seoul 2, Gyeonggi 4).
- All pages intentionally use `noindex,nofollow`; no canonical and no production host binding.
- Shared images are referenced at `/assets/images/{hero,shop-in-shop,menu,street,ingredients}.webp`. Binary assets are not yet tracked in Git; HTML must not be considered visually complete until those files are added and verified.
- Margin is described as sales minus ingredient cost (gross margin), not net profit. Entry-cost and purchasing/contract details are omitted and routed to phone consultation.
- The region names were matched to the historical V5 inventory; this is not a claim of current coverage or local business conditions.
